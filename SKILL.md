# Weekly Summary Writer

## Purpose
Give the leader a calm, short overview of the week.

## Inputs
The results from the intervention flagger for all employees across the last 7 to 8 days.

## Rules
- Keep it under 200 words.
- Use neutral, factual language. No blame, no labels about character.
- Every statement must be backed by the data. If data is thin, say so.

## Structure
1. Overall picture: how many employees reported consistently and how many were flagged.
2. Consistent and healthy: who reported fully and with results.
3. Needs attention: who, and in one line why.
4. Issues that keep repeating: items appearing across several days or several people.
5. Suggested next steps for the leader, in two or three lines.

## Output
The summary in short paragraphs. Offer to save it as a Gmail draft if the leader wants to share it.
